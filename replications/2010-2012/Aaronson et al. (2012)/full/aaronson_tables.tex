\noindent\textbf{Replication status:} Partial replication (CPS analysis only).
The full paper has three data sources: CPS, CEX (Consumer Expenditure Survey), and SIPP.
The CPS regressions (Tables 1, A1, A3) were replicated from the supplemental files.
CEX and SIPP analyses require additional data preparation programs
(\texttt{addotherprogs.do}, \texttt{otherstateprogs.dta}) not fully available.
